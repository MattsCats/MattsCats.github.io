Synopsis

A categorical perspective on physical and computational processes

My current research focuses on theories of higher-order operations: processes that transform other processes. I am interested both in their abstract categorical organization and in how they behave in concrete physical settings, especially quantum theory.

This includes work on higher-order quantum objects, supermaps, causality, spacetime interventions, generalized process theories, and reward-based models for planning and learning in quantum and multi-agent systems.

Current Focus

  • Higher-categorical foundations for higher-order operations
  • Quantum information, locality, and indefinite causal structure
  • Generalized probabilistic and infinite-dimensional settings
  • Agent policies, actions, observations, and planning
