Assistant Professor in Computer Science specialising in applied category theory and quantum foundations. My research develops categorical foundations for higher-order operations, with applications to quantum information theory, quantum causality, and the broader study of agent-environment interaction.

I am interested in the mathematical structure of higher-order operations, including their organisation into higher-categories and notions of equivalence between them. I also work on applications to infinite-dimensional, field-theoretic, and generalised probabilistic systems, as well as reward-based models of planning and learning in quantum and multi-agent settings.
