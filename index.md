Assistant Professor in Computer Science specialising in applied category theory and quantum foundations. My research develops categorical foundations for higher-order operations and agent-environment interaction in finite dimensional quantum theory and beyond.

<img src="/Screenshot%202026-08-01%20at%2022.22.56.png" alt="Matt Wilson giving a talk" style="width: 36%; min-width: 220px; max-width: 300px; float: right; margin: 0.5rem 0 0.75rem 1.25rem;">

Beyond developing the abstract algebra of higher-order operations, including their organisation into higher-categories and notions of equivalence between them, I am also actively working on applying those abstract representations to infinite-dimensional, field-theoretic, and generalised probabilistic systems, as well as reward-based models of planning and learning in quantum and multi-agent settings.
